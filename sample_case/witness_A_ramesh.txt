STATEMENT OF WITNESS u/s 180 BNSS
Name: Ramesh Gupta, shopkeeper, Central Market, Lajpat Nagar.

On 15 March 2024 I was closing my shop. At around 9 PM I saw Rajesh Verma arguing with two men
near the parking lot. One of them was tall and wore a red jacket; I know him as Vikram Singh.
I heard Vikram say "pay back my money today". I then went inside to count the cash.
At about 9:20 PM I heard a scream and saw a man in a red jacket running towards the main road.
I did not see anyone holding a knife. I called the victim's wife after seeing him on the ground.
The street light near the parking was not working that night.
