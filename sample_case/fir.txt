FIRST INFORMATION REPORT
FIR No. 214/2024, Police Station Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi
Date of registration: 15 March 2024, 23:40 hrs
Sections: 302, 34 IPC (corresponding Section 103, 3(5) BNS)

Complainant: Sunita Verma, wife of the deceased Rajesh Verma.

The complainant states that on 15 March 2024 at about 9 PM her husband Rajesh Verma left home
to meet one Vikram Singh near Central Market regarding a money dispute of Rs. 2,00,000.
At about 10:30 PM she received a call from a shopkeeper that her husband was lying injured
near the market parking. He was taken to AIIMS Trauma Centre where he was declared dead.
She suspects Vikram Singh and his associate Mohit Yadav, with whom her husband had quarrelled
the previous week. A knife was reportedly recovered near the spot.
